About

Hi, I am Yaoyang.

I’m currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science, Peking University, under the supervision of Prof. Yinjun Wu. Before that, I obtained my bachelor’s degree from the School of Information, Renmin University of China in 2025.

My research focuses on algorithms for machine learning systems and vector retrieval. I am interested in improving the efficiency of ML workloads and developing scalable vector and multi-vector retrieval methods.

我目前是北京大学计算机学院博士研究生， 导师为吴垠鋆教授。 此前，我于 2025 年在中国人民大学信息学院获得学士学位。

我的研究聚焦于面向机器学习系统的算法与向量检索，致力于提升机器学习工作负载的效率，并探索可扩展的向量与多向量检索方法。
