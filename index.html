About

Hi, I am Yaoyang.

I am a Ph.D. student at Peking University. Before that, I studied Data Science and Big Data Technology at Renmin University of China.

My research interests lie in machine learning systems, information retrieval, and cloud computing. I am particularly interested in building efficient systems for large language models and retrieval-augmented generation.
